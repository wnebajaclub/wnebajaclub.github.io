This Season’s Focus

Project GBR4

GBR4 marks the fourth Baja vehicle designed and built by Golden Bear Racing since the team’s start in 2013—and it’s set to be our most capable yet. Built on over a decade of experience, GBR4 is being engineered to push the limits of what our team has accomplished, with the goal of becoming the highest-performing vehicle in Golden Bear Racing history.
